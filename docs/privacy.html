Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Open Source Project

1. Overview & Commitment

Fanta OFME Downloader is an open-source, non-commercial desktop application developed by ZuhuInc. We are strictly committed to user privacy. We do not operate tracking services, we do not sell or harvest user data, and we do not run third-party advertisement networks.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Fanta OFME Downloader, with specific details on our Google Drive Cloud Saves & Sync feature.

2. Google API Data & Permissions (drive.file)

When you connect your Google account to enable cloud game save synchronization, Fanta OFME Downloader requests the following minimal Google OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Per-file access to files created or opened by Fanta OFME Downloader
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — Used exclusively to display your account name inside the app UI so you know which account is active

Strict Limited Access Guarantee

Because we exclusively use the drive.file scope, Fanta OFME Downloader can ONLY access the game backup zip files and folders that the app itself creates (under the folder Fanta OFME Cloud Saves). The app has ZERO access to your personal Google Drive documents, photos, sheets, emails, or existing Drive files.

How Google Drive Data is Used:

  1. Creating Backup Archives: Compresses your local save folder for a chosen game into a zip archive and uploads it to your personal Google Drive inside a dedicated folder named Fanta OFME Cloud Saves.
  2. Restoring Saves: Downloads the backup zip archive you created back to your local computer and restores it into your game save directory.
  3. Checking Save Timestamps: Compares the timestamp of your cloud backup against your local game files to alert you if a newer cross-PC save is available.

3. No Third-Party Servers or Data Transfers

Fanta OFME Downloader communicates directly between your personal computer and Google's official API servers (googleapis.com).

  • No Intermediate Servers: Your game save files, credentials, and OAuth tokens never pass through or get stored on any server owned or controlled by ZuhuInc or any third party.
  • Local Storage Only: OAuth tokens and user preferences are stored strictly on your local machine in your private user profile directory (e.g. CloudSync.json).
  • Zero Telemetry: We do not log, track, or analyze your game saves, playtime, or download habits.

4. Data Control & Revoking Access

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnect Account: You can disconnect your Google account at any time in the app under the Cloud & Saves tab by clicking Disconnect. This immediately purges all stored tokens from your computer.
  • Delete Cloud Saves: You can open your Google Drive at any time and permanently delete the Fanta OFME Cloud Saves folder or any individual game zip archives.
  • Revoke Google Permissions: You can revoke access at any time via your Google Account Security Dashboard.
